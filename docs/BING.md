# Bing Web Master

## Setup

### 1. Add site to Bing Web Master

1. Go to [Bing Web Master](https://www.bing.com/webmaster/).
2. Click **Get Started**.
3. Login with Google account.
4. You will see two options. Choose the Left Option: **Import your sites from Google Search Console**.
5. Click **Import**.
6. Click **Continue** to give Bing read access to your verified sites.
7. Authorize Bing to access your Google account.
8. Select **https://haolamnm.dev/** from the list and click **Import**.
