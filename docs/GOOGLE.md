# Google Search Console

## Setup 

### 1. Add site to Google Search Console

1. Go to [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console).
2. Login with Google account.
3. Click **Add a property**
4. Select **URL Prefix** (Right side).
5. Enter `https://haolamnm.dev`.
6. Click **Continue**.
7. Choose the **HTML tag** method.
8. Add the `<meta>` tag to `index.html`.
9. Push new changes to GitHub.
10. Wait for GitHub Actions to finish.
11. Click **Verify**.

### 2. Add domain to Google Search Console

1. Go to [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console).
2. Login with Google account.
3. Click **Add a property**
4. Select **Domain** (Right side).
5. Enter `haolamnm.dev`.
6. Click **Continue**.
7. Choose the `Name.com` method.
8. Authorize Google to access your `Name.com` account.
9. Let Google verify your domain.
